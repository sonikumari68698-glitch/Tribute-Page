01 — THE PERSON

More Than a Scientist

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was a scientist, teacher and former President of India.

He dedicated his life to science, education and inspiring young people. His simple personality and powerful vision made him one of the most admired personalities in India.

His journey reminds us that our background does not decide our future — our dreams, dedication and actions do.
